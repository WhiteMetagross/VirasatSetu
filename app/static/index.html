How Virasat Setu Works

A transparent, secure four-stage process connecting verified families with financial institutions.

1

Submit Record

Citizen submits deceased family details, death cert ref and identity reference (like PAN).

2

Admin Verification

Civil registrar records and death certificate are verified by administrator before discovery begins.

3

Automated Matching

Weighted algorithm searches Banks, Insurance and Mutual Funds (Exact ID 50, Name 25, DOB 15, Nominee 10).

4

Claim and Payout

Heir submits relationship proof; institutional representative processes and updates claim status.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this prototype access live bank accounts or transfer real money?

No. As specified in the Project Scope (Section 13), this is an academic working prototype developed with fictional datasets (40+ bank records, 25+ insurance, 25+ mutual funds). It simulates the complete legal and technical workflow without touching restricted or live financial networks.

How does the 100-Point Weighted Matching Algorithm work?

The matching engine queries across all financial datasets evaluating four weighted criteria: Sample Identity Ref (PAN/Aadhaar) = 50 pts, Name Token Similarity = 25 pts, Exact Date of Birth = 15 pts, and Nominee Family Relationship/City = 10 pts. Scores of 80-100 generate Strong Potential Matches; 60-79 require manual review; below 60 are discarded.

How do notifications and emails work in this system?

The system generates both in-app database notifications and simulated official email dispatches logged with full headers, timestamps, and recipient alerts. You can inspect the email body directly from the notification bell icon!
