# Virasat Setu Operational Usage Manual:

This document provides a thorough operational guide for citizens, government administrators and financial institution representatives using the Virasat Setu platform.

Virasat Setu End to End User Journey:
1. Citizen Registration: Citizen signs up or logs into portal.
2. Submit Deceased Civil Record: Enters death certificate details and PAN.
3. Government Admin Verification: Admin approves record and triggers auto matching.
4. Automated Discovery Engine: Evaluates bank, insurance and fund datasets.
5. Discovered Assets Display: Citizen reviews matched assets and scores.
6. Legal Heir Claim Initiation: Submits claimant capacity and documentation.
7. Institution Representative Desk: Bank or insurer reviews claim and issues decision.
8. Transparent Status Tracking: Citizen monitors five stage visual progress bar.


## Citizen and Family User Workflow:

Citizens and legal heirs follow an intuitive self service workflow to discover family assets:

1. Portal Authentication: Citizens access the portal and sign in using registered credentials or select demo family login for instant exploration.
2. Deceased Person Registration: The user navigates to Register Deceased tab and submits personal information including full name, birth date, death date, gender and last known city.
3. Official Certificate Details: The user inputs civil registration details including death certificate number, issuing municipal authority and identity reference.
4. Claimant Declaration: The submitter provides their own name, contact details, relationship with the deceased and claimant capacity as legal heir or nominee.
5. Document Attachment: The citizen attaches digital copies of municipal death certificates or legal succession proofs.
6. Initial Record Queuing: Upon submission the record enters Pending Verification status and awaits administrative civil review.
7. Discovered Assets Exploration: Once verified the citizen visits the Discovered Assets tab to review potential bank accounts, insurance policies and mutual fund folios.
8. Claim Initiation: The citizen clicks Initiate Claim on identified assets, confirms claimant details and provides supporting remarks.
9. Lifecycle Tracking: The citizen monitors claim progress through the Claim Tracking console with real time status transitions.


## Government Administrator Workflow:

Government administrators oversee civil validation and platform security through a dedicated command center:

1. Administrative Login: Authorized officers access administrative controls via secure credentials or quick access switcher.
2. Platform Overview: The administrator dashboard displays global metrics including total registered citizens, active financial records, matching counts and pending claims.
3. Civil Certificate Review: Officers examine incoming deceased person submissions against official municipal records.
4. Verify and Auto Match Action: With a single click the administrator approves the death certificate and immediately triggers the automated one hundred point matching engine across all banking, insurance and fund databases.
5. Record Rejection or Document Requests: In cases of discrepancy the administrator can reject submissions or flag records for additional documentation.
6. Central Financial Record Registration: Administrators can add new financial assets directly to the central database across banks, insurers and mutual funds.
7. Audit Log Inspection: The administrator reviews an immutable chronological audit trail tracking user logins, matching executions and claim determinations.


## Financial Institution Representative Workflow:

Institutional desks provide segregated operational consoles for nodal claim officers:

1. Institution Authentication: Representatives log in using institution specific credentials like LIC claims desk or SBI settlement office.
2. Contextual Desk Routing: The platform routes officers directly to their assigned institutional desk without exposing unrelated records.
3. Assigned Claims Queue: Officers view incoming claim requests matching assets held within their specific institution.
4. Claimant Verification Review: Officers inspect claimant declarations, succession credentials and uploaded death certificates.
5. Official Status Determination: Officers update claim status across standard lifecycle stages including Under Review, Documents Required, Approved and Completed.
6. Official Nodal Remarks: Officers append binding instructions, payout details or documentation requisitions visible to the claimant.


## Browse Central Database Workflow:

Citizens and officers can explore central institutional repositories transparently:

1. Navigate to Browse Database: Click Browse Database from the main navigation header or home hero banner.
2. Comprehensive Repository View: Explore ninety four indexed records encompassing forty two bank deposits, twenty six insurance policies and twenty six mutual funds.
3. Category Filtering: Filter records by clicking category pills for Bank Accounts, Insurance Policies or Mutual Funds.
4. Instant Search Filtering: Enter citizen names, account references or identity numbers in the search field with dynamic filtering.
5. Detailed Asset Metadata: Review asset types, institution names, holder names, birth dates, approximate valuations and dormancy states.


## Event Notification and Email Dispatch Inspection:

Virasat Setu ensures complete operational transparency through real time alerts:

1. In Portal Notification Center: Citizens click the top bell icon to inspect incoming alerts regarding asset discoveries and claim approvals.
2. Unread Badge Counter: A visual indicator highlights unread alerts and updates automatically.
3. Simulated Email Dispatch Inspector: Users can click Inspect Simulated Email Dispatch inside notification details to review official transactional emails formatted with government headers.
