# Virasat Setu Official Brand Identity and Logo Design Specification:

This document establishes the conceptual foundation, geometric construction, symbolic anatomy and chromatic system for the official visual identity of Virasat Setu.

Virasat Setu represents an essential piece of digital public infrastructure. The visual emblem must project sovereign authority, institutional credibility, financial integrity and compassionate warmth for citizens navigating bereavement.


## Core Symbolism and Conceptual Foundation:

The identity unites two core concepts derived from the name:

1. Virasat (Legacy and Generational Wealth): Represents the accumulated savings, insurance protections and investments built by ancestors for their families. The visual identity honors this legacy as an enduring foundation that rightfully belongs to surviving legal heirs and nominees.
2. Setu (The Bridge of Discovery): Represents the digital public infrastructure that connects separated entities. The bridge spans the divide between citizens, municipal death registries and institutional financial vaults without administrative friction.

The emblem synthesizes these principles into an inspiring, memorable and sovereign mark that commands immediate trust across banking desks, municipal counters and citizen smartphones.


## Visual Anatomy and Symbolic Motifs:

The emblem comprises four interconnected visual motifs harmonized into a balanced geometric crest:

1. The Sovereign Bridge Arch: A graceful upward sweeping curve symbolizes Setu. The arch spans horizontally across the upper crest, creating an architectural shelter that conveys protection, structural stability and cross sector connectivity.
2. The Kalpavriksha Sprout: Rising directly beneath the bridge shelter is an elegant three leaf seedling in polished gold. In Indian cultural heritage the Kalpavriksha symbolizes the wish fulfilling tree of wealth and life continuity. In this context it represents recovered financial inheritance blossoming into new prosperity for bereaved families.
3. The Concentric Continuity Ring: A subtle semi circular curve anchors the base of the emblem, referencing the 24 spoke wheel of national law and perpetual continuity. It signifies that financial rights do not dissolve upon death, but transfer lawfully to the next generation.
4. The Interconnected Data Apex: The crest of the bridge incorporates three geometric circular nodes linked by subtle energy paths. This signifies the tripartite integration between banking repositories, insurance databases and mutual fund houses.


## Color Palette and Psychological Impact:

The color system draws from sovereign national symbolism paired with financial prestige:

1. Sovereign Navy Blue (Hex 0F2942): Forms the dominant structural arch. It communicates institutional authority, data privacy, constitutional legitimacy and cybersecurity resilience.
2. Radiant Saffron (Hex E06612): Accents the dynamic bridge curve. Saffron symbolizes dignity, warmth, civic service, sacrifice and auspicious beginnings for families in distress.
3. Growth Emerald (Hex 138808): Accents the foundation base and supporting arcs. Green represents financial renewal, legal resolution, fertility and transparent governance.
4. Prestige Heritage Gold (Hex D4AF37): Coats the central sprout and wealth nodes. Gold visually denotes recovered monetary wealth, financial liquidity and ancestral security.
5. Pristine White (Hex FFFFFF): Provides the breathing background field, ensuring total contrast and accessibility across high resolution displays and print media.


## Typography and Bilingual Wordmark:

The wordmark balances national heritage with modern typographic readability:

1. Primary Devanagari Wordmark: Set in bold, geometric letterforms for विरासत सेतु. The lettering features open counters, balanced horizontal shirorekha alignment and solid stem weight that conveys permanence.
2. Secondary Latin Wordmark: Set in a modern geometric sans serif typeface like Montserrat or Inter for Virasat Setu. The uppercase letters feature wide tracking for optimal legibility at micro scales.
3. Subtitle Classification: Accompanied by a clean descriptor line in small capital letters reading National Unclaimed Financial Asset Discovery Platform.


## Layout Adaptations and Lockup Formats:

The emblem adapts across diverse digital and physical contexts:

1. Primary Horizontal Lockup: The circular crest is positioned on the left side with the bilingual typography stacked neatly on the right side. This format serves as the primary header on the web portal, navigation bars and official documentation.
2. Centered Formal Crest: The emblem is centered prominently above the bilingual wordmark and official government descriptor. This format is utilized on formal certificates, annual platform reports and municipal signage.
3. Minimalist App Icon and Favicon: A distilled silhouette featuring only the sovereign arch and golden central sprout within a circular navy tile. This maintains crisp visibility even at sixteen pixel favicon dimensions.
4. Single Color Stencil Variant: A pure black or solid white version engineered for high contrast physical stamping, municipal seal presses and single color letterheads.


## Geometric Construction and Grid Rules:

1. Circular Bounding Proportion: The emblem is constructed within a strict golden ratio circle divided into eight equal radial segments.
2. Arch Thickness: The outer bridge curve maintains a stroke thickness equal to one tenth of the total emblem diameter.
3. Protective Clear Space: A mandatory breathing perimeter equal to the height of the central gold sprout must surround the logo on all sides, free from text or graphics.
4. Minimum Display Dimensions: The full horizontal lockup must never appear smaller than one hundred and twenty pixels wide on digital screens or thirty millimeters in physical print.


## Application Across Platform Touchpoints:

1. Web Portal Header: Positioned in the upper left corner of the national portal, instantly signaling legitimacy and sovereign security.
2. Transactional Email Communications: Rendered in high resolution at the apex of simulated email dispatches, assuring claimants that alerts are official government communications.
3. Municipal Physical Posters: Printed on informational posters displayed across municipal birth and death registration offices to guide bereaved citizens.
4. Institutional Settlement Consoles: Displayed alongside participating bank logos on institutional processing desks like State Bank of India and Life Insurance Corporation.
